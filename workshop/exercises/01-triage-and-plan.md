# Exercise 1 (8 min): Triage request and execution plan

## Objective
Act as the primary agent and define a safe, scoped delivery plan for a fictional feature request.

## Real-world prompt
"Add a simple Community Support page section for fictional Banco Vecinal by end of day."

## Do this
1. Review governance/instruction sources:
   - `/README.md`
   - `/docs/agent-ecosystem.md`
   - `/mcp/README.md`
   - `/mcp/policies/approved-tools.md`
2. In your notes, define:
   - Scope you will implement directly in `/workshop/starter/app.js`
   - What you would delegate to each specialist (frontend/test/security/documentation)
   - One operation that would require human approval

## Expected outcome
A clear primary-agent plan with explicit boundaries and governance awareness.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Plan includes direct work vs delegated work.
- [ ] Plan references approved-tool/least-privilege thinking.
- [ ] At least one approval-gated action is identified.

## Reflection checkpoint
Why is separating “do now” from “delegate/review” useful under time pressure?
